Why I teach this

I was the patient before I was the teacher.

I'm Dr. Katie To. I run the Center for Integrated Wellness and Cosmetic Dentistry in Katy, Texas, and I chair the International Academy of Biological Dentistry and Medicine. Before any of that, I had my own recovery arc around mercury toxicity, and it changed how I thought about the mouth forever. I stopped seeing a tooth and started seeing a system.

Every component of the Accelerator is what I wish someone had handed me earlier. The biocompatible foundations, the consult, the identity. The Wellness Dentist Institute exists because other dentists kept asking how I did it, and a flat list of courses was never the right answer.

If you have been listening to the podcast, this is the next step. Mentorship, not another course.
